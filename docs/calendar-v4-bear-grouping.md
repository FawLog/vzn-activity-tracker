# Calendar v4.4: recurring Bear grouping
In Add event / Advanced settings, choose Bear Trap, minute precision, a locally verified fixed interval of 1–7 days (including 47.5 or 48 hours), and enable Group recurring Bear traps. Enter the same first UTC cycle start for related trap series in every alliance, a distinct trap identity for each, and the actual scheduled trap time. Preview and review before saving. Cycle dates derive from the shared anchor plus the recurrence offset, not the individual trap's UTC date. Group identities persist during renewal. No attendance, sessions or actual events are created by grouping.
Conflicts with existing slots, incompatible auto-group anchors/cadence on the same cycle day, duplicate host/time, or finalized cycles reject initial creation or pause renewal. Finalization checks missing expected materializations of active/paused series as well as existing grouped scheduled occurrences. Individual reviewed exceptions remain authoritative. Edit schedule supports a single future occurrence or a reviewed suffix. Time edits keep the configured shared cycle identity, including moving before the original cycle anchor within one interval. The rolling cursor and duration are recalculated; other traps, history, cancelled dates and manual exceptions remain untouched. Fixed cadence changes split the series at the selected occurrence, preserve occurrence IDs, and update future renewal. Cadence changes with other grouped traps, manual exceptions or insufficient consecutive future slots are rejected rather than silently breaking grouping. Future cancellation preserves cancelled slot identity and stops renewal. Existing finite month/weekday series retain their cadence and shift existing dates; changing that cadence is not supported. No automatic migration or guessing of existing Bear schedules. Templates deliberately do not preserve date-specific group anchors. Uses existing series rule metadata, no new migration.
