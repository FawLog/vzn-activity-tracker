# Calendar v4.4: recurring Bear grouping
In Add event / Advanced settings, choose Bear Trap, minute precision, a locally verified fixed interval of 1–7 days (including 47.5 or 48 hours), and enable Group recurring Bear traps. Enter the same first UTC cycle start for related trap series in every alliance, a distinct trap identity for each, and the actual scheduled trap time. Preview and review before saving. Cycle dates derive from the shared anchor plus the recurrence offset, not the individual trap's UTC date. Group identities persist during renewal. No attendance, sessions or actual events are created by grouping.
Conflicts with existing slots, incompatible auto-group anchors/cadence on the same cycle day, duplicate host/time, or finalized cycles reject initial creation or pause renewal. Finalization checks missing expected materializations of active/paused series as well as existing grouped scheduled occurrences. Individual reviewed exceptions remain authoritative. Future cancellation preserves cancelled slot identity and stops renewal; independent series shifts are blocked because they can break cross-alliance alignment. Cancel and replace with a reviewed anchor when changing the pattern. No automatic migration or guessing of existing Bear schedules. Templates deliberately do not preserve date-specific group anchors. Uses existing series rule metadata, no new migration.
