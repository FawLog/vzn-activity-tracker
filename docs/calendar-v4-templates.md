# Calendar v4.3: alliance templates
Saved named templates are scoped to one alliance and group. They preserve event type, precision, UTC time, duration, phase, recurrence parameters and rolling preference. The sample date is not a new event: applying rebases to the current editor date and resets confidence to estimated. The normal preview/validation and save flow still applies. Templates can be saved or deleted in Advanced settings; reusable templates appear in Add event after selecting an alliance. No events, attendance or results are created by template CRUD. Existing series are unaffected by deleting templates. Current scope is single-event templates, not automatic bulk creation of a full alliance week or multi-phase cycle; those require further reconciliation and grouping work.
